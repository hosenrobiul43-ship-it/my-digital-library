লেখক: Jibonanondo Das জীবনানন্দ দাশ

Jibanananda Das was a Bengali poet, writer, novelist and essayist. Dimly recognised during his lifetime, today Das is acknowledged as “the premier poet of the post-Tagore era in India" and Bangladesh. One of his translators, Clinton B. Seely, is among those who consider Jibananda Das as "Bengal's greatest modern poet and its "best loved poet" too. Here we present a collection of all of his poems. জীবনানন্দ দাশ বিংশ শতাব্দীর অন্যতম প্রধান আধুনিক বাংলা কবি। তাঁকে বাংলাভাষার "শুদ্ধতম কবি" বলে আখ্যা
